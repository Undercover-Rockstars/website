The suit is not the disguise.

The person in the meeting is real. So is the person at 2am. We stopped pretending one of them was the costume.

02

Everything comes in pairs.

One pattern, cut twice. A day version and a night version that share every seam. You buy both, and you never go home to change.

03

Quiet from ten metres. Loud from one.

Details live where only the people close to you will see them. A lining. A seam. A line of text stitched black on black.

04

Nothing changes but the light.

The same jacket. The same shirt. What shifts is the room, the hour, and how much of yourself you decide to show.

05

Undercover is a choice.

Not a hiding place. Being undercover means you have something worth uncovering, and you decide when.

06

Everyone has a rockstar undercover.

Founders. Designers. The person across the table. This is for the version of you that shows up when the deck is closed.
