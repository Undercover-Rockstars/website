05 / Codes

Quiet from ten metres.
Loud from one.

Code 01

One pattern, two fabrics

Every pair is cut from a single block. The day and night versions share every seam, so the fit never changes when the world does.

Code 02

Sold together, worn apart

A pair is one product, one size, one price. The day piece goes to the meeting. The night piece waits in the bag.

Code 03

Black on black

Lyrics, marks and monograms stitched tone on tone. You have to be close to read them.

06 / The tag

Every piece knows
who it belongs to.

An NFC tag sits in the seam of all sixteen garments. Hold a phone against it and the piece answers. No app to install, nothing to open, nothing printed on the outside.

Tag 01

Proof it is real

Every garment is registered to its pair before it leaves Bali. One tap confirms the piece, the pair it was cut with and the drop it came from. A copy can borrow the look. It cannot borrow the tag.

Tag 02

Lost at 4am

Jackets go missing at the rave. Whoever finds yours taps it, writes one line, and it reaches you. They see that the piece is spoken for. They never see your name, your number or your address.

Tags travel with the garments. Drop 01 has not shipped, so there is nothing to tap yet.
